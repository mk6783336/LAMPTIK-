Questions that come up every week

How do I download a TikTok video without a watermark?

Copy the link in TikTok (Share → Copy link), paste it above, open the downloader. TikTok keeps two files: the clean play master for its own player and a stamped wmplay copy for sharing. Request play and there is no watermark to remove — cropping or blurring a logo only cuts into the frame.

Why is a downloaded 1080p video silent?

Because high-resolution YouTube streams are often video-only, with audio in a separate file. A downloader that grabs the video stream alone writes an MP4 with no audio track. Muxed H.264 + AAC is the file you want; if 1080p only exists as VP9/AV1, a 720p muxed file with sound beats a silent “HD” one.

Do I need an app or an account?

No. Everything here runs in the browser you already have, including on iPhone — Safari saves to Files, and Files can save the clip to Photos.

Is the file re-compressed?

No. The bytes come from the platform's own CDN; nothing is transcoded on the way to you. That also means the quality ceiling is whatever the upload exposed — a 720p upload cannot produce a 1080p file.
