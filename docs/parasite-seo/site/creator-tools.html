TikTok money (RPM) calculator

“TikTok pays X per 1,000 views” is a range presented as a rate. The arithmetic that matters is views ÷ 1000 × RPM, minus the 24% US withholding if you are not a US taxpayer.

  • Put your own numbers in and see the split rather than a single headline figure.
  • Separates For You traffic from search traffic, which pay differently.
